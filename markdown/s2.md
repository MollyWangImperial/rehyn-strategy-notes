# Earnings long straddle

`s2` · Short term volatility · Options

## Why the opportunity exists
### The Structural Opportunity: Underpriced Earnings Moves

The investment case rests on the tendency of options to price smaller earnings reactions than some companies deliver, which allows buyers of straddles to earn a return from the minority of announcements that cause large moves. The pattern persists because option prices rely on the typical past reaction, while changes in guidance, trade policy and regulation can move individual stocks far more than that average.

The main risk is a steady loss of premium when announcements produce moves smaller than the options imply. Most straddles expire with a small loss, so the return depends on a few large winners and can be negative for several quarters in a row. Rehyn estimates that results are weakest when implied volatility is already high across the market, because straddles then cost more and the stock must move further to cover the premium.

## What Real Market Shocks Reveal About This Strategy

The large figure on each card shows the share price move on the first trading day after results. Options on large US companies usually price a move of about 4% to 8% on results day, so the straddle gains when the actual move is larger and loses when it is smaller.

Netflix reported fourth-quarter subscriber growth well above expectations in January 2013, and the shares rose about 42% the next day. The straddle gained because the move was far larger than the move options had priced.

**February 2022 · Platform policy · −26% · strategy gained**

Meta Platforms warned of weaker revenue in February 2022 and cited Apple's privacy changes, and the shares fell about 26% in a day. The straddle gained because the fall was far larger than the move implied by option prices.

**April 2022 · Geopolitics · −35% · strategy gained**  
Russia exit contributes to Netflix subscriber loss  
Netflix reported its first subscriber loss in a decade in April 2022, a result it linked partly to suspending service in Russia, and the shares fell about 35%. The straddle gained because the decline was much larger than the move options had priced.

**November 2023 · Steady results · −0.5% · strategy lost**  
Apple shares barely move after results  
Apple reported a small fall in quarterly revenue in November 2023, and the shares closed about 0.5% lower the next day. The straddle lost because the move was far smaller than the 3% to 4% that options typically price for Apple.

**April 2025 · Health policy · −22% · strategy gained**  
UnitedHealth guidance cut follows higher medical costs  
UnitedHealth cut its 2025 profit forecast in April 2025 after higher medical costs in its Medicare Advantage business, and the shares fell about 22% in a day. The straddle gained because the move was several times the size options had priced.

**August 2025 · Export policy · −1% · strategy lost**  
Nvidia shares barely move after results  
Nvidia reported record revenue in August 2025 and excluded sales to China from its forecast because of US export licensing, yet the shares closed about 1% lower. The straddle lost because the move was much smaller than the move options had priced.

## Quick backtest across 16 years of market shocks

Return 13.2% · volatility 16.8% · Sharpe 0.58 · worst fall −21% (April 2020)

- Period: January 2010 to December 2025; 192 monthly observations
- Instrument: Single-stock options on US large-cap stocks; at-the-money straddles expiring within a week of results
- Rules: Straddles bought one to three days before results when the implied move is below the stock's average earnings move over the past eight quarters; closed after the announcement; exposure capped per stock
- Costs: Estimated bid-ask spreads and commissions deducted on entry and exit

## Strategy construction, from underpriced earnings moves to limited premium loss

| Position | Instrument | Purpose | Allocation |
|---|---|---|---|

| Long put, at the money | Single-stock options | Gains when the stock falls by more than the premium paid | 3% premium |
| Treasury bills | US Treasury bills | Provides collateral and earns the cash rate | 94% |

- Entry: Implied move at least 20% below the stock's average earnings move over eight quarters
- Position size: Premium capped at 0.25% of capital per stock and 6% in total
- Exit: Position closed at the next market open after results
- Stop-loss: No price stop; loss limited to the premium paid

### Today’s market watch

The size of implied earnings moves compared with the average reaction over recent quarters is the main indicator for this strategy. Trade policy statements, changes in company guidance and the busiest weeks of the reporting season matter because they change both the price of options and the size of the moves. Rehyn also watches how many companies have options pricing a smaller move than their past eight quarters delivered.

## Performance drivers and the geopolitical risks that matter most

**Favourable conditions**

- **Guidance changes surprise investors**: Large guidance changes favour the strategy because they produce moves well beyond those implied by option prices. Netflix in January 2013 rose about 42% after results, several times the move options had priced.
- **Policy shifts reach company earnings**: Policy changes favour the strategy when they alter a company's revenue outlook without warning, because option prices rely on past reactions. UnitedHealth in April 2025 fell about 22% after a profit forecast cut linked to rising medical costs, which is several times a typical reaction.
- **Low option prices before results**: Low option prices before results favour the strategy because the premium paid is small compared with the moves that can follow. Nvidia in May 2023 rose about 24% after raising its forecast for chip demand, far above the move options had priced.

**Adverse conditions**

- **Expensive options before results**: High option prices before results hurt the strategy because the stock must move further than usual to cover the premium. Nvidia in August 2025 closed about 1% lower after results, well short of the move options had priced.
- **Stable results with small reactions**: Predictable results hurt the strategy because stocks with steady earnings rarely move by more than the options imply. Apple in November 2023 closed about 0.5% lower after results, so the premium paid decayed with little offsetting gain.
- **Market-wide stress lifts premiums**: Market-wide stress hurts the strategy when it lifts option prices for every stock at once, so straddles cost more even where results are routine. In spring 2020, implied volatility across US stocks was far above its long-run average, which made each straddle expensive.

## Where Else Can Investors Find Event Volatility Returns?

- **Variance risk premium harvesting** (`s1`): Sells index options and earns premium from calm markets. Bears crash risk, losing when volatility rises sharply. *Takes the opposite side of volatility*
- **Long gamma scalping** (`s4`): Owns index options and hedges them frequently. Bears time decay, losing steadily when markets move less than priced. *Continuous version of the same exposure*
- **Post-earnings announcement drift** (`e2`): Trades the drift in prices after results. Bears reversal risk when initial reactions fade instead of continuing. *Acts after results, not before*
